\documentclass{article}

\usepackage{booktabs}
\usepackage{graphicx}
\usepackage{amsmath}

\title{[Paper Title]}
\author{[Anonymous for review]}
\date{}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
State the problem, method, evidence summary, and key outcome in 4-6 sentences.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\textbf{Problem.} Describe what fails today and why it matters to software engineering.

\textbf{Claim.} Provide one sentence: ``We introduce X, which achieves Y under Z.''

\textbf{Contributions.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Contribution 1 (method/idea)
  \item Contribution 2 (evidence/result)
  \item Contribution 3 (artifact/reproducibility impact)
\end{itemize}

\section{Method}
Describe the approach, assumptions, and key design decisions.

\section{Experimental Setup}
Datasets/projects, baselines, metrics, and statistical protocol.

\section{Results}
Use one subsection per research question. Tie each subsection to a table/figure.

\section{Threats to Validity}
\textbf{Construct validity.}
\textbf{Internal validity.}
\textbf{External validity.}
\textbf{Conclusion validity.}

\section{Related Work}
Organize by approach line, not paper-by-paper narration.

\section{Conclusion}
Re-state claim, strongest evidence, and limitations.

\bibliographystyle{plain}
\bibliography{references}

\appendix
\section{Replication Package}
List scripts, environment details, and exact commands for all reported results.
